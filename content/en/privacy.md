## 1. Who is responsible

This website (www.ankosoftlab.com) is operated by **Individual Entrepreneur Anton Kozyrev (Georgia), ID 304822565, trading as AnKo Software Labs** (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question, contact us at [contact@ankosoftlab.com](mailto:contact@ankosoftlab.com).

This policy covers the website only. Our products (for example TapReply or Terroir) have their own privacy policies on their own websites.

## 2. What this website does not do

- It does **not** set cookies and does not use local storage.
- It does **not** use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking tools.
- It does **not** load fonts, scripts or other resources from third-party servers.
- It has **no** contact forms, accounts or newsletters.

## 3. Hosting and server logs

The website is a static site. It is currently hosted on **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc.) and may later be moved to **Cloudflare Pages** (Cloudflare, Inc.). When you open a page, your browser sends a technical request to the hosting provider. The provider may process and temporarily log data such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested page, the referring page and your browser’s user agent, in order to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse.

We do not use these logs to identify visitors and do not combine them with other data. The hosting providers process this data under their own privacy policies and may do so outside your country of residence.

## 4. Email

If you write to us by email, we receive your email address, your name if you provide it, and the content of your message. We use this information only to answer you and, if we start working together, to communicate about the project.

- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries, or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
- **Retention:** correspondence is kept as long as needed for the conversation and for any resulting business relationship and legal obligations (for example accounting), and then deleted.
- We do not sell or share your messages with third parties, except with our email service provider, which stores the mailbox on our behalf.

## 5. External links

The site links to the websites of our products (tapreply.net, terroir-app.com). Those websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, email [contact@ankosoftlab.com](mailto:contact@ankosoftlab.com). You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority — in Georgia, the Personal Data Protection Service.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy when the website changes (for example, when hosting moves to Cloudflare Pages). The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
